# First beta: 1,000 RU/EN questions

The owner selected a 1,000-question milestone before TestFlight. The previous 310 questions remain unchanged. This release adds 690 original bilingual catalog-fact questions in 11 categories, bringing delivery to 1,000 questions in 32 playable categories. The longer-term 10,000 / 50 target is separate; 18 categories still have no playable pack.

## Primary factual references

- 340 individually selected World Heritage properties: [UNESCO World Heritage List / DataHub](https://data.unesco.org/explore/assets/whc001/). Each question links to its property page. Only brief property names and discrete facts (State Party, initial inscription year, category) are used to write new questions. Descriptions, feed documents, images, logos and videos are not republished. No blanket CC license or endorsement by UNESCO is claimed. Syndication permission is not asserted. Transboundary and mixed properties, problematic names, Iguaçu name ambiguity and overseas-country ambiguities are excluded.
- 60 distinct paintings: [Art Institute of Chicago collection API](https://api.artic.edu/docs/), factual metadata designated CC0 by the museum. Title + museum date + collection disambiguate a work. One named artist is required; images and narrative descriptions are not included.
- 90 distinct literary works: [Project Gutenberg's catalog](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/feeds/pg_catalog.csv). Work titles, author identities, certain author dates and two subject facts are checked. Translations/editors are not mistaken for authors; repeated editions are excluded. No ebook text, cover or generated Gutendex summary is copied. Ebook upload dates are never described as original publication dates.
- 30 natural satellites: [NASA/JPL physical-parameter table](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/phys_par/). Parent body and approximate mean radius, not diameter. Pluto is described as a celestial body, never an eighth/ninth planet in these questions.
- 30 constellation abbreviations: [IAU table](https://www.iau.org/IAU/Astronomy-FAQs/Constellations.aspx). Nominative, abbreviation, genitive; no star charts copied.
- 50 distinct chemical elements: [IUPAC table hosted at Queen Mary University](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/AtWt/). Symbol, name and proton/atomic number. Mutable atomic weights are not used. Earlier symbol/name question pairs are not repeated in reverse.
- 40 currencies: [ISO 4217 maintenance agency SIX, List One](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/market-reference-data/data-standards.html). Alphabetic and numeric codes; no exchange rates or financial guidance.
- 50 named network services: [IANA service/transport registry](https://www.iana.org/assignments/service-names-port-numbers/). Every prompt specifies service and transport, preventing ambiguous same-number registrations. Explanations include port-range classification and the distinction between registration and actual traffic.

Territory and currency labels use Unicode CLDR through Babel, with editorial corrections of acronym case and Kyrgyzstani/Kazakhstani names. [Full Unicode License V3](UNICODE-LICENSE.txt) is retained and bundled by the app. These proper-name facts and original question templates do not claim a license to entire external catalogs. Existing Global-MMLU adaptations retain their separate Apache credit/notice.

## Actual review scope

`authoring/beta1000_choices.py` explicitly selects 350 named science/culture/currency/network entities. A bounded geography shortlist adds 340 single-country properties, with a per-country cap. Draft generation **never writes approvals**. Source snapshots are pinned by SHA-256 in `beta1000.source-hashes.json`; selected factual records and their hashes are in `beta1000.evidence.json`.

A separate Codex pass inspected selected source-record projections and RU/EN template/label pairs, corrected candidate IDs, author parsing, malformed source HTML, stale/mismatched property names and ambiguous geographic scope. Distinct option classes/identities and one canonical answer were checked; structural checks are additional evidence, not substitutes for factual reading. The accepted ID/content-hash list is `beta1000.reviewed-selection.json`, and each question has a review tied to that hash. This is one agent's source/wording review, not independent expert or human review. `humanApproved` remains false.

The beta broadens playable content but is intentionally weighted toward geographic, literary, art, scientific and technical knowledge available from directly checkable primary records. It does not pretend that all 50 directions are populated, nor that this is the final editorially accepted App Store catalog. Before public release, the owner should assess difficulty and thematic balance in play.
